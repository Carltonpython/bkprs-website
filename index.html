Our Origin

Built on Decades of Consistency and Trust

BKPRS Limited was established in the 1980s by founder Alvin Whyte. What began as a dedicated practice resolving complex payroll administration and statutory tax filing hurdles for local enterprises has evolved steadily over forty years.

As client operations expanded and regulatory demands modernized, BKPRS scaled its infrastructure to deliver the full spectrum of technical accounting, financial reporting, and external assurance.

1980s: Foundation

Pioneering dedicated payroll accuracy, statutory records, and tax compliance.

Growth & Evolution

Expanding capabilities into complete corporate bookkeeping, financial compilation, and advisory.

Present Day: Full-Gamut Practice

Multidisciplinary director-led leadership delivering modern assurance and enterprise financial governance.
